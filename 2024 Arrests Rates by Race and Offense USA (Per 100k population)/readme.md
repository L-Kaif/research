# 2024 Arrest Rates by Race and Offense

This table presents **2024 arrest rates** **per 100k population** calculated from **FBI arrest counts** and **U.S. Census population estimates**.

---

## Notes

- **AI/AN** = American Indian/Alaska Native
- **NH/OPI** = Native Hawaiian/Other Pacific Islander
- Arrest data are from **2024**.
- Rates are **not age-adjusted**.
- Rates are rounded to **two decimal places**.
- Population denominators are from the **U.S. Census Bureau** and may differ from the population coverage used in **FBI arrest-rate calculations**.
